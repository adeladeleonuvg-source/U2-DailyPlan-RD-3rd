Plan #1

Amazing Animal Senses

Source: Our World 3 Student Book (pp. 72, 73, 74)

CLASS TIME ALLOCATION 50 Minutes Total

Moment I: Warm-Up & Brain Break (~5 mins)

Goal: Activate prior knowledge, energize students, and introduce core reading context.

Moment II: Guided & Individual Reading Task (20 mins)

Goal: Promote independent reading comprehension and active text processing (anti copy-paste).

Moment III: Cooperative Team Activity (30 mins)

Goal: Peer interaction, assigned team roles, and concrete output creation.

Moment IV: Plenary & Interactive Quiz/Game (15 mins)

Goal: Share solution routes, verify learning through quick interaction, and final evaluation.

Plenary Interactive Checklist (Summative / Formative Group Assessment)

Rubric / Checklist
Evaluation Criteria Excellent (3 pts) Satisfactory (2 pts) Needs Improvement (1 pt) Score Tracker
1. Subject Mastery & Oral Clarity Explains reading concepts clearly using unit vocabulary. Explains main ideas with minor vocabulary errors. Struggles to express basic ideas or vocabulary.
2. Logic & Solution Procedure Shows clear step-by-step logic and evidence from text. Shows answers but lacks strong text evidence. Procedure is unclear or incomplete.
3. Collaborative Work & Equity All team members participate with assigned roles. Most members participate active in discussion. Only one student presents or does all work.
4. Response to Plenary Questions Responds accurately and confidently to teacher questions. Responds with partial assistance or prompt. Unable to answer verification questions.
TOTAL SCORE: 12 / 12 points
